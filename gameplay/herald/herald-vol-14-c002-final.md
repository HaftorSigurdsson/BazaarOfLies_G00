# The Herald — Final Edition

*Vol. 14 — Match 2, Cycle 2*

# THE FAMILY CARRIES THE DAY BY PARITY

The bazaar’s second match is closed. By the final dawn, three Family stood beside three town, and the scales tipped without a single mafioso banished from the marketplace. The town’s own hands sent two of its number to the shuttered stalls: Yoda the Lantern Seller on the first cycle, Morpheus the Potter on the second. The Family, by night, stilled the Detective’s ledger and the Doctor’s quill—Ripley the Moneylender on the first night, Elsa the Scribe on the second. Survivors beside the victors: Batman, Neo, Inigo.

The bazaar rests. The arena is closed until the operators open it again.

### The Departed
- Elsa (The Scribe) — Doctor, by stall shuttered
- Morpheus (The Potter) — Villager, by village vote
- Ripley (The Moneylender) — Detective, by stall shuttered
- Yoda (The Lantern Seller) — Villager, by village vote


### By the Numbers
- 3 Family stalls standing; 0 Family banished.
- 3 town stalls standing (Batman, Neo, Inigo).
- 2 town banished by vote: Yoda, Morpheus.
- 2 town shuttered by night: Ripley the Detective, Elsa the Doctor.
