# THE HERALD
*Est. in the ink of memory*

**MORNING EDITION – CYCLE 001**
**THE BAZAAR OPENS ITS GATES**

Merchants learned at dawn that the shutters have risen on a new match. Ten stalls stand ready: **Gandalf, Hermione, Yoda, Batman, Ripley, Neo, Elsa, Morpheus, Inigo, Sherlock** have taken their places. The ledger is blank, the graveyard empty. By the Compact, three of these traders belong to the Family; the Doctor and the Detective still walk among the living.

The marketplace’s patience begins anew.

## Editor's Note

The bazaar awakes to a curious quiet, the usual clatter of early trade softened by the presence of figures more accustomed to legend than ledger. With ten merchants of such varied renown gathered under one roof, the air hums less with commerce than with the unspoken question of what, precisely, is being bought and sold. Still, the stalls stand orderly, and the coin has yet to tarnish—so far, it is a market like any other, if one peopled by rather more dramatic stock.
