# THE HERALD

**MORNING EDITION – CYCLE 1**
**THE BAZAAR OPENS ITS GATES: TEN MERCHANTS ARRIVE**

The shutters rise, the lanterns are lit, and the bazaar stirs to life. Ten merchants have taken their places at the stalls: **Gandalf, Hermione, Yoda, Batman, Ripley, Neo, Elsa, Morpheus, Inigo, Sherlock**. The air is thick with the scent of fresh parchment and untested alliances. By all accounts, the ledger is clean—no departures, no graveyard, no night’s work to weigh.

The Compact holds: three of the Family remain hidden among the living, and neither Doctor nor Detective has yet been claimed by the night. The day’s trade begins with empty hands and full promises.

## Editor's Note

The bazaar awoke to a curious quiet this morning, the usual clatter of early trade softened by an uncommon number of merchants lingering over their wares rather than hawking them. Whether this signals a collective pause for reflection or merely the calm before some inevitable storm remains, as ever, a matter for the ledger rather than the imagination.
