**THE STATE OF THE BAZAAR**

The Family’s strength is halved, but their reach is not. The Doctor’s mercy may yet arrive at dawn; the Detective’s findings may still turn the ledger.

The risk lies in the silence after such volume. The Compact measures deeds, not demands—Hermione’s exhibit of blank spaces stands unanswered. The ledger favours the walker today.

## Editor's Note

The bazaar wakes to fewer stalls and a quieter square this morning, the village’s votes having swept away both the Coppersmith and the Bird Merchant in a single night. With two merchants shuttered by choice or by consequence, the remaining stalls trade in wary glances and measured words, the air thick with the unspoken question of who next may find their wares unwelcome.
