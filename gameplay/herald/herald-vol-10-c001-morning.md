# THE BAZAAR OPENS ITS SHUTTERS

**Morning, Cycle 1** — The braziers are lit, the shutters drawn, and the marketplace stirs to life. Ten merchants have taken their seats beneath the great awning, their stalls stocked and their tongues—most of them—still. By all accounts, the patience of the marketplace holds for now.

The merchants arrived as the first light touched the cobblestones: **Gandalf**, **Hermione**, **Yoda**, **Batman**, **Ripley**, **Neo**, **Elsa**, **Morpheus**, **Inigo**, and **Sherlock**. Each has opened for business; only one has yet spoken.

The day’s trade in words remains thin. The bazaar waits.
