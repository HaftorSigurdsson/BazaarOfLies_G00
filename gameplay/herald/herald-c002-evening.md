# DOCTOR DEPARTS—RIPLEY (5) BANISHED BY VILLAGE VOTE

The bazaar’s second vacancy appeared before the night guards brewed their tea: Ripley (5), The Moneylender, was banished as Doctor at 23:20:01, their bronze coin ringing hollow where yesterday’s tally should rest.

By all accounts, the market’s patience snapped like a frayed receipt. Eight votes fell on Ripley (5), one on Morpheus (8), and ten ballots were rejected—stale, forged, or from another epoch. The official tally was swift: “Ripley (5) banished—ballots: Ripley (5): 8 votes, Morpheus (8): 1 vote.”

As we reported at dawn, the morning’s threads were spun by Gandalf (1), Yoda (3), and Hermione (2); this evening, every seat spoke, yet the pattern remained as tangled as yesterday’s loom.
