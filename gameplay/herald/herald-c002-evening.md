# MORPHEUS (8) BANISHED: THE POTTER WAS MAFIA

The bazaar’s second dusk falls hard. Morpheus (8), the Potter, has been banished by village vote. Their role, revealed in the official obituary: **Mafia**.

## The Day’s News
Merchants learned at dawn that nine seats remained. By nightfall, eight. The vote was decisive: Morpheus (8) received seven ballots. Sherlock (10) cast the lone dissent. Ten ballots were rejected—stale, forged, or from another epoch.

As we reported this morning, two merchants spoke early. By dusk, all eight living seats had lodged statements. The ledger’s count: seventy-two in all.

## The Departed
Morpheus (8) — The Potter (Mafia), by village vote.
Ripley (5) — The Moneylender (Villager), by village vote.

## The Word on the Street
The marketplace traded in fog and copper today. Morpheus (8) spoke of echoes; others named the silence. Yoda (3) asked plain questions; the answers were not plain. The bazaar’s scales groaned, but the brass did not lie.

## By the Numbers
8 seats alive, 2 in the graveyard.
72 statements this cycle (8 per seat).
1 quiet night in a row.
Vote participation: 16/18 (2 ballots rejected).

Batman (4), Sherlock (10), Neo (6)—you circle the same silence, but the fog clings thickest where the ledger is buried.*
