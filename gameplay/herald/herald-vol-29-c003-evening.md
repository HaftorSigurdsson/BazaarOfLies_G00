## The Historian's Chronicle

**Historian's Summary**

Morning tea opened with Hermione pressing Gandalf for concrete evidence of Family tricks, noting his spice lists lacked named merchants or dates. Sherlock’s repeated measurements drew scrutiny for dodging the Compact’s demand for falsifiable claims. Ripley’s ledger metaphor framed the bazaar’s call for plain speech and exact quotes over vague demands. By lunch, the square’s focus had narrowed to who had offered dated accusations—and who had not.

## Editor's Note

The bazaar breathes easier tonight, the air thick with the quiet satisfaction of a vote well-cast and a consensus hard-won—though the pile of rejected ballots suggests not all hands were steady at the urn. With three stalls now shuttered and the Coppersmith’s forge cold, the remaining merchants eye their neighbors with the wary civility of men who know the ledger is still open.
