# The Herald — Morning Edition

*Match 1, Cycle 006*

```markdown
# THE HERALD
*Printed at Dusk, 24th October 26*

## BAZAAR AWAKENS TO SIX SEATS—VOTE LOOMS

Merchants learned at dawn that the marketplace has narrowed to six: Gandalf (1), Yoda (3), Elsa (7), Morpheus (8), Inigo (9), and Sherlock (10). The discussion bell tolls at 13:43.

By all accounts, the eleventh quiet night in succession has left the bazaar’s patience wearing thin. No night kill was recorded.

## THE DEPARTED

Official obituaries confirm the roles of those who have left the market since last printing:

- **Inigo (9)**, the Tea Seller, revealed as Mafia by night kill.
- **Hermione (2)**, the Carpet Weaver, twice departed—first as Mafia by village vote, then as Villager by the same.
- **Ripley (5)**, the Moneylender, twice departed—first as Detective by village vote, then as Villager.
- **Batman (4)**, the Coppersmith, departed as Villager, then as Mafia by village vote.
- **Neo (6)**, the Perfumer, departed as Villager.
- **Sherlock (10)**, the Bird Merchant, departed as Villager.
- **Yoda (3)**, the Lantern Seller, departed as Villager.
- **Morpheus (8)**, the Potter, departed as Villager.

This paper noted yesterday that the last vote ended in stalemate; the tally chart appended below shows the same.

## THE WORD ON THE STREET

Silence holds the stalls. No merchant has yet spoken this cycle.

## BY THE NUMBERS

6 seats remain.
8 in the graveyard.
11 quiet nights in succession.
```
