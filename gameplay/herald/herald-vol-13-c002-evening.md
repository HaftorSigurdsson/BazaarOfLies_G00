# THE HERALD — Evening Edition

*Vol. 12 — Match 2, Cycle 2*

# MORPHEUS BANISHED — VILLAGER, BY VILLAGE VOTE

Evening, Cycle 2 — The bazaar’s second dusk brings a second banishment. Morpheus, the Potter, is gone—cast out by the village vote, their role revealed as Villager. The marketplace exhales; the ledger closes on another account.

Seven merchants remain: **Gandalf**, **Hermione**, **Batman**, **Neo**, **Elsa**, **Inigo**, and **Sherlock**. The kiln’s fire dims; the night guards brew their tea.

As we reported at dawn, the tongs have gripped once more.

---

### The Departed
Morpheus (The Potter) — Villager, by village vote.

---

### The Word on the Street
The day’s trade in words turned on iron and silence. Morpheus spoke of hollow measures and rusted tongs; others pressed for plain names. The bazaar’s fire melted shadows to truth—or tried to. By all accounts, the patience of the marketplace frayed where the kiln’s coals needed names, not chirps.

The discussion closed on absence, not answers.

---

### By the Numbers
7 seats alive, 3 in the graveyard.
72 statements this cycle.
Morpheus banished — 5 votes to 2.

---

### Letter to the Editor
From Seat 2:

*Hermione: The loom hums loudest where the shuttle hides, Morpheus (8), yet seat-08’s kiln still breathes iron fumes sharper than yesterday’s rust. Batman (4), your tongs grow heavy with silence—did the Doctor’s cloak slip through your fingers, or did the Family snatch it first? Sherlock (10), four lantern swings at seat-07 and not one plain name—Ripley shuttered, Yoda banished, and the bazaar’s fire melts hollow measures to ash.*
