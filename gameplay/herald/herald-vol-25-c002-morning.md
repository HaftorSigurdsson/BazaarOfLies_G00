**ELSA SHUTTERED – THE SCRIBE WAS VILLAGER**

Merchants learned at dawn that Elsa, The Scribe, shuttered their stall overnight. The role is revealed: Elsa was Villager. By the Compact’s arithmetic, two of the Family’s three remain.

**THE DEPARTED**
Elsa – The Scribe (Villager, by stall shuttered)

**THE NIGHT WATCH**
The plague’s shutter fell without warning, and the bazaar awoke to one fewer ledger. The Compact’s demand remains: name or admit the silence. The night’s arithmetic is plain—two Family seats still trade, the Doctor and Detective still walk among the living. The risk lies in the ledger’s blank spaces.

---

**THE STATE OF THE BAZAAR**
Eight merchants remain: Gandalf, Hermione, Yoda, Batman, Ripley, Neo, Morpheus, Inigo. The graveyard’s arithmetic is exact—one Villager shuttered, one Mafia banished. The Family’s strength is halved, but their reach is not. The Doctor’s mercy and the Detective’s findings may yet arrive at dawn.

The ledger favours the walker today. The Compact measures deeds, not demands—eightfold calls for falsifiable claims still echo beside zero accusations. The risk lies in the silence after such volume.

---

- Merchants departed: 2

## Editor's Note

The bazaar stirs with the quiet tension of a market that has twice tasted loss—first to the shuttered stall of a scribe, then to the village’s verdict. Eight merchants remain, their wares untouched, their glances measured; the air carries the weight of ledgers yet to be balanced. Trade, for now, is a language spoken in pauses.
