# THE HERALD — Evening Edition

# YODA BANISHED — VILLAGER, BY VILLAGE VOTE

Evening, Cycle 1 — The bazaar’s first blood is drawn. Yoda, the Lantern Seller, is gone—banished by the village vote, their role revealed as Villager. The marketplace exhales; the ledger closes on one account.

Merchants learned at dusk that the tongs had gripped plain wares after all. By all accounts, the patience of the marketplace held until the kiln’s fire cooled.

---

### The Departed
Yoda (The Lantern Seller) — Villager, by village vote.

---

### The Word on the Street
The day’s trade in words turned on silence and scent. Ripley spoke of iron filings in the air; Gandalf of bolts and alloy. Inigo demanded plain answers; Morpheus, plain wares. The bazaar’s scales tilted where the tongs refused to grip, and the Family’s pattern—folded rugs, shuttered stalls—hummed beneath every ledger.

The discussion closed on knots, not stitches.

### Letter to the Editor
