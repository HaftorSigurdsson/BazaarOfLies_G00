# SHERLOCK (10) BANISHED: THE BIRD MERCHANT WAS VILLAGER

The bazaar’s third dusk brings a reckoning. Sherlock (10), the Bird Merchant, has been banished by village vote. Their role, revealed in the official obituary: **Villager**.

## The Day’s News
Seven seats remain. The vote was swift: Sherlock (10) received seven ballots. Elsa (7) cast the lone dissent. Eighteen ballots were rejected—stale, forged, or from another epoch.

As we reported at dawn, the marketplace spoke in full today. Sixty-four statements were lodged, eight from each living seat. The ledger’s count is now complete.

## The Departed
Sherlock (10) — The Bird Merchant (Villager), by village vote.
Morpheus (8) — The Potter (Mafia), by village vote.
Ripley (5) — The Moneylender (Villager), by village vote.

## The Word on the Street
The bazaar traded in copper and silence today. Sherlock (10) spoke of ledgers and creditors; others named the fog. Gandalf (1)’s censer still chimes, but the flock’s hunger is no riddle. The scales groan, but the brass does not lie.

## By the Numbers
7 seats alive, 3 in the graveyard.
64 statements this cycle (8 per seat).
2 quiet nights in a row.
Vote participation: 24/30 (6 ballots rejected).

---
*Letter to the Editor*
**From Sherlock (10):**
*The bazaar’s lanterns gutter like a ledger’s last page, and the spice stalls hum with the weight of unpaid copper. Elsa (7), your quill still beads ink like a Potter’s last breath—but the Potter’s rot clings to *your* silence. You asked Hermione (2), “Did the Potter’s fingers tremble too close to the ledger’s flame?” yet your own page stays blank. A debt without a name is a shadow without a coin. When the creditor’s name is written in unpaid tears, whose tears ink *your* ledger? Did the Potter’s fingers tremble too close, or did you choose the blade to cut the debt without naming the hand that held it?*
