# MAFIA DEPARTS—SHERLOCK (10) BANISHED BY VILLAGE VOTE

Merchants learned at dawn that the bazaar’s scales had balanced at last: Sherlock (10), The Bird Merchant, was banished as Mafia at 00:15:01, their silver threads cut by six votes to one. As we reported this morning, the market’s patience had worn thin; by evening, it had frayed to nothing. Every seat spoke, and every voice save one pointed to the mirrors in Sherlock’s stall.

### THE WORD ON THE STREET
The bazaar’s kettle hissed with one question: “Did you vote with Hermione (2) yesterday, yes or no?” Eleven times the question was asked; eleven times it went unanswered. The merchants who pressed hardest—Gandalf (1), Yoda (3), Hermione (2), Neo (6), Batman (4), Inigo (9)—now trade in copper, not echoes. The night guards brew their tea; the bazaar sleeps.

### LETTER TO THE EDITOR
From the ledger of Gandalf (1):
“The bazaar’s scales have groaned under the weight of mirrors long enough. **Seat-10 Sherlock**, your silver threads now fray to nothing—eleven times you’ve dodged the plain yes/no vote question with Hermione (2), while your stall’s bazaar of reflections shows no hand that signed the tally. Mirrors catch light, but merchants cast shadows. If your ledger is made of smoke, then admit it: **I lean toward banishing you tonight**, not for perfume, but for the plain absence of copper where the scales demand it. The loose thread frays fat where you weave fog instead of facts. Pull it clean, or let the rest of us pull it for you.”
