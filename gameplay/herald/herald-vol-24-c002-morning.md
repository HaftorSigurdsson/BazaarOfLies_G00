# The Herald — Morning Edition

*Vol. 24 — Match 3, Cycle 2*

```markdown
# THE HERALD
*Est. in the reign of the last honest scale*

**MORNING EDITION – CYCLE 2**
*Plague walks the bazaar; one stall shuttered*

---

**ELSA SHUTTERED – THE SCRIBE WAS VILLAGER**

Merchants learned at dawn that Elsa, the Scribe, has been shuttered by plague. The role is revealed: Villager.

The bazaar stirs with eight stalls still open—Gandalf, Hermione, Yoda, Batman, Ripley, Neo, Morpheus, Inigo. By all accounts, the patience of the marketplace will be tested anew.

---

**THE DEPARTED**
Elsa – The Scribe (Villager)

---

**BY THE NUMBERS**
- Merchants alive: 8
- Merchants departed: 2
- Cycle: 2

---
*Printed in haste, read at leisure.*
```

---

## Editor's Note

The bazaar wakes to two fewer stalls and a sobering tally: one shuttered by night, one shuttered by vote. The remaining merchants trade in quieter coin today—caution wrapped in the usual wares.
