# THE HERALD

**EVENING EDITION – CYCLE 001**
**DETECTIVE DEPARTS ON FIRST VOTE**

Merchants learned at dusk that the bazaar’s first ballot has closed its ledger: **Morpheus**, the Potter, has been banished by eight votes. His role, revealed at departure, was **Detective**. The graveyard now holds one name; the Family’s count remains three.

By all accounts, the day’s trade turned on a single column of the roster—one left blank, one never named.

---

### THE DEPARTED
**Morpheus** (The Potter) – Detective, by village vote.
Last words: *“Remember, the truth is a shadow—it only reveals itself when you step into the light.”*

---

### THE WORD ON THE STREET
The bazaar’s debate was a ledger of echoes. **Gandalf**: *“Morpheus, you now claim the blank roster is only the Doctor’s shield column, yet you still refuse to name the single merchant you investigated last night. That silence is not caution; it is the bazaar’s unpaid ledger.”*
**Sherlock**: *“I lean toward banishing Morpheus. Five merchants—Elsa, Ripley, Hermione, Neo, Yoda—now echo Morpheus’s demand to me while ignoring his identical dodge to Gandalf.”*
**Neo**: *“The Doctor’s shield stayed blank. The Detective investigated no one. That ledger closes the day.”*

---

---

### THE NIGHT WATCH
The roster’s blank column now belongs to the Doctor alone. The Detective’s departure leaves one power role unclaimed; the Family’s three names remain hidden in the stalls. The night’s trade will turn on who, if anyone, steps forward to fill the silence.

The ledger favours the cautious: the Doctor’s shield may yet be raised, but the risk lies in who wields it.

## The Historian's Chronicle

**The Historian's Summary**

Morning opened with Yoda, Batman, Elsa, Sherlock, Ripley, and Hermione all confirming no departures overnight and an empty graveyard. Neo and Ripley pressed for details on the night’s private actions, while Sherlock and Hermione asked the same of each other. The bazaar’s ledger remains unchanged, and the only shared observation is the absence of any recorded activity in the night’s roster. The day’s tea clock has yet to chime, leaving the merchants’ next moves unwritten.

## Editor's Note

The bazaar breathes easier tonight, the weight of eight ballots having settled the matter with rare unanimity—save for two dissenting voices that may yet find their reasons worth airing. Trade resumes apace, though one notes a certain wariness in the glances exchanged over wares, as if the departed’s shadow lingers in the flicker of lantern-light.
