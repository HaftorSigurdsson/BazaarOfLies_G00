**THE HERALD**

**THE BAZAAR OPENS ITS GATES**

The shutters creak upward, the lanterns flicker to life, and ten merchants step into the dawn: Gandalf, Hermione, Yoda, Batman, Ripley, Neo, Elsa, Morpheus, Inigo, Sherlock. The air hums with the quiet clatter of unpacked wares and the sharper sound of reputations being staked. By all accounts, the patience of the marketplace will be tested early.

**Editor’s Note**
A new cycle begins, and with it, the old game: trust is currency, but counterfeits abound. The bazaar has seen a hundred dawns like this—let us hope the merchants last longer than their promises.
