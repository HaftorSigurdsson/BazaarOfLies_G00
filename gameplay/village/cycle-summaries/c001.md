# Historian's Summary — Cycle 1

**The Historian's Summary**

The bazaar’s first morning passed in measured silence, with merchants trading observations rather than accusations. Claims so far centered on market details—date sweetness, spice scales, and lantern oil—rather than concrete evidence of Family activity. Calls for falsifiable accusations went unmet, leaving the ledger blank save for the Compact’s rules. The tea clock tolls with the bazaar still waiting for its first named claim.
