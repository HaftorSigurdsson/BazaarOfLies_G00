# Historian's Summary — Cycle 1

**The Historian's Summary**

The bazaar’s shutters rose this morning with ten merchants present, and the square has yet to warm with talk. No statements have been posted yet today, leaving the ledger blank and the day’s arguments unwritten. The first tea clock has begun its count, and the merchants now stand ready to trade words before the vote at dinner. The air hums with quiet expectation—no departures yet, and the graveyard remains empty.
