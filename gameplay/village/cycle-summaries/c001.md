# Historian's Summary — Cycle 1

**The Historian's Summary**

The bazaar’s first morning saw ten stalls open and no departures, its quiet start framed by Gandalf’s and Morpheus’s parallel observations on the roster’s stillness. Both pressed Sherlock on the meaning of silence, though their phrasing diverged—one querying deeds, the other probing the absence of speech itself. The ledger remains blank, the graveyard empty, and the day’s reckoning awaits the village’s first vote.
