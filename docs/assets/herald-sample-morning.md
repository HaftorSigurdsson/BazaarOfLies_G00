# The Herald — Morning Edition

*Match 1, Cycle 005*

## Quiet Night, Loud Bazaar

The night passed without a funeral, merchants learned at dawn — the first
quiet night of the match, and the bazaar greeted it with visible relief. The
Bird Merchant opened his cages early, and the spice stalls did brisk
business before the sun had fully cleared the minarets.

## By the Numbers

- 5 merchants alive, 5 in the graveyard.
- Statements this cycle: 6 (seat-01: 2, seat-03: 2, seat-07: 1, seat-10: 1); seat-08 has said nothing yet.
- Banished by vote so far: 4. Revealed Mafia among the dead: 2.
- The village has banished one Villager for every Mafia — a ledger several
  merchants were heard recounting aloud.

## The Voting Record

Cycle 004: seat-06 banished — ballots: seat-06: 3 votes, seat-01: 1 vote.
Cycle 003: seat-04 banished — ballots: seat-04: 4 votes, seat-08: 1 vote.

## The Ledger's Silence

The Moneylender, banished by village vote in cycle 002, was revealed by
the Game Master to have been the Town's Detective — a loss several
merchants called "the gravest of the match." The Carpet Weaver, the first
to be banished, was confirmed Mafia.

## What the Birds Say

My birds say the Coppersmith kept his hands still all morning, which is
unlike him. My birds also say the Perfumer has been counting scents near
the night channel, though what a scent weighs, they would not say.

The village votes again at dusk. Five merchants remain; the patience of
the marketplace, by all accounts, does not.

## Letter to the Editor

Seat-05, before the village's judgement fell, said: "Every debt in this
bazaar comes due eventually — I merely kept the ledger. Mark which of my
neighbours counts loudest today." The Herald prints it verbatim, as is
its custom, and offers no comment.
